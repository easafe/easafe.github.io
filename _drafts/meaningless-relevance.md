Nihilism is bad. Mostly everyone knows what it entails; mostly everyone disagrees with it. A flip side to nihilism - that comes often disguised as good news or facts, and less well known - is toxic positivity. That is, anything ever said by Steven Pinker. The World Bank reports. Toxic positivity looks at what we have achieved with glee, ignores what we have not in this "we live in the best possible world" fashion that is not interested at all to question that "possible" bit lest it be whining and ungratefulness. Cheerful ignorance is not the answer to apathy.

However, as a pair counterpart, there are two other things that I would like to call _nihilist relevance_ and _meaningless positivity_.
